Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Los Angeles Post (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and shares information when you visit this website. By using the website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. If you contact us by email, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the website, we and our partners automatically collect certain information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed, time spent on pages, video playback activity, links clicked, and campaign parameters included in the link you used to reach us (for example, UTM parameters).

Payment information. We do not collect or store payment card information. Purchases are completed on the checkout page of a third-party vendor, which handles your payment and order details under its own privacy policy.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies to operate the website, measure traffic, understand how visitors use our pages, measure the performance of our advertising, and attribute sales to the correct marketing channel.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block cookies, some parts of the website may not work as intended.

3. Third-party services we use

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) – to measure website traffic and usage. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Advertising platforms (such as Google Ads and Meta) – to measure and improve the performance of our ads.
  • Video hosting (vturb / ConverteAI) – to deliver the video presentation and measure playback statistics.
  • Campaign tracking and affiliate attribution tools – to measure which ads and links lead to visits and purchases, so that sales can be credited correctly.
  • Product vendor and payment platform – when you click a purchase link, you leave our website and the vendor's checkout collects the information needed to process your order.

4. Advertising and remarketing

We use advertising services, including Google Ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or, in the European Union, www.youronlinechoices.eu.

5. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website;
  • To measure traffic and the performance of our advertising campaigns;
  • To attribute purchases made through our links and receive affiliate commissions;
  • To respond to your messages and requests;
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your name, email address or other contact information. We share information only with the service providers and partners described above, when required by law, or to protect our rights. Some sharing of device and browsing information with advertising partners may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under certain U.S. state laws; you can opt out as described below.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other U.S. states, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, email contact@losangelespost.online with the subject line “Privacy Request”. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where required by law.

8. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International visitors

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate, which may have data protection laws different from those in your country.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
